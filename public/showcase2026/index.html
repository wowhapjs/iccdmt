ICC CAMPUS REPORT · 09 OCT 2026

Digital Media Technology Student Showcase,
17 student creators shared their work with an invited audience.

DATE
09 October 2026
TIME
10:30–11:30
VENUE
Ikirezi Hall
PROGRAM
Faculty remarks · team introductions · screenings · fellowship

Opening with faculty remarks

The program opened with faculty remarks and final encouragement to the student creators before the public presentation of their work.

Three teams, three video works

Three student teams introduced their production process and presented completed videos: a market-life documentary, a community-focused story about Glory Academy, and a production story tracing the journey from idea to screen.

Screening, reflections, and shared response

The invited audience watched the films together, giving the students an opportunity to experience how completed media work changes when it moves from the editing room into a shared public space.

A well-attended time of refreshments and fellowship

The formal program continued informally as students, faculty, and guests remained for refreshments, conversation, and feedback after the screenings.

With three completed videos and 17 student creators at the center of the program, the showcase concluded successfully as a dynamic media event where student work was presented, discussed, and celebrated in front of a live audience.
